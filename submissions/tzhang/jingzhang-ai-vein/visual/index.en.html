Three-Tier Scope

The proposal advances through the coordination study scope, overall design scope and key-area scope, binding industry judgement, urban renewal and key-district design to layers and metrics.

project_scope_summary.csv

Coordination Study Scope · 43.6 km²

Proposes an AI industry ecosystem, three-district two-wing coordination, future urban form and cultural narrative for the full territory.

  • University origination - open-source collaboration - enterprise conversion innovation chain
  • 5-8 global AI innovation ecosystem benchmark cases
  • Brand naming and communication system (AI Vein VI)

Overall Design Scope · 11.4 km²

Organizes urban renewal, land-use structure, transport and utilities, and the Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality belt within 1-2 km around the heritage park.

  • Regulatory-plan-depth urban design (full coverage of 16 parcels)
  • Retain - renew - rebuild - reserve framework
  • AI Vein blue-green slow-way system

Key-Area Scope · 368.4 ha

Detailed design for three key districts, specifying functional programme, building renewal, public space and transport organization.

  • Zhongzhi Park - Zhichuangyuan (192 ha R&D origination)
  • Origin Community - Zhihuigu (104 ha conversion & open source)
  • Dazhongsi - Zhixiangcheng (72 ha scenario application)

Key Areas

All three key areas carry industry positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space moves, transport interfaces and implementation-risk notes.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhi Park AI Independent-Innovation Accelerator

Zhichuangyuan · full-stack independent innovation district. Emphasizes national platforms, industry display, Qinghe culture, external transport and low-carbon exchange space.

  • Qinghe innovation parlor + waterfront green wedge
  • Independent-model test field / standards workshop
  • North Fifth Ring feeder arterials

Beijing AI Origin Community

Zhihuigu · campus-adjacent conversion district. Emphasizes university origination, open-source community, talent services, retain-demolish-rebuild decisions and station integration.

  • Open-source plaza + public code wall + contributors wall
  • Campus - park - street three-zone stitching
  • Lightweight first-phase facilities first

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Zhixiangcheng · urban intelligent-economy district. Emphasizes leading enterprises, agentic new business forms, commercial services, composite use of green space and four-quadrant intersection connectivity.

  • Four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity at the station
  • Smart terminal display / international roadshow
  • Southern gateway east-west linking arterial
